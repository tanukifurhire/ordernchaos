ORDER N CHAOS - DESCRIPTION

Order N Chaos is a turn-based tactical duel fought across two grids at once.

In the world of Oudain, humanity harvested the spiritual energy lingering in
places heavy with memory, and gave form to the myths it found there. Every
harvest came at a cost. The drained places soured, corrupting whatever they
touched into something sinister, and humanity gave the result a name: Chaos.
The survivors raised floating fortresses called Skycastles above the ruined
land. Chaos seized what it could reach and twisted them into Dreadcastles.
From those fortresses the two sides now bombard each other across the sky.

You command three champions on your own six-by-four grid while your opponent
commands theirs. Each turn, both players plan at the same time and under a
timer, spending action points to move units and queue abilities onto tiles of
the enemy board. Nothing resolves until both sides lock in. Then the resolution
phase plays out every queued action in order, and you find out whether you read
your opponent correctly or spent the turn shelling ground they had already left.

Where your units stand matters as much as what they cast. Every ability demands
a number of red and blue tiles held by your own units, so positioning is what
unlocks your arsenal. Tiles crack and break as they take fire, and a board you
have been bombarding all match eventually stops supporting the abilities that
depend on it. Before deployment you build each unit's loadout from its signature
abilities, a pool of shared generics, and a passive, then take that squad into a
duel where a burn, a shield or a well-timed recast decides the exchange.

FEATURES

* Simultaneous turns - both players plan blind against a ninety-second timer,
  then watch every queued move and ability resolve together in sequence.

* Two factions, six champions - Dragoon, Oracle and Tirion fight for Order;
  Imp, Eye and Vizom for Chaos. Each brings two signature abilities, a passive,
  and status effects such as Burn, Bleed, Shield, Fervor, Wrath and Berserk.

* Positional casting on a breakable board - abilities are gated by the red and
  blue tiles your units occupy, and the tiles themselves degrade under fire.

* Pre-match loadout drafting - assign two active abilities and one passive to
  every unit before deployment, and build a squad around how you like to play.

* Two ways to duel - online 1v1 matchmaking through Steam, or shared-screen
  pass-and-play on a single machine, with a guided interactive tutorial to learn
  the flow of a turn.
