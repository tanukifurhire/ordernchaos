ORDER N CHAOS - PLAYERS

Play modes supported:

    Multi-Player        Yes  (always 1 vs 1)

MULTI-PLAYER MODES

    PVP - Local         Yes
                        "Pass and Play" mode. Two players share one machine
                        and one screen, handing the mouse over between turns.

    PVP - LAN           No

    PVP - Online        Yes
                        Two players on separate machines, matched through a
                        Steam lobby browser and connected peer-to-peer over
                        Steam Networking Sockets. One player hosts, the other
                        joins from the in-game server browser.

    Co-op - Local       No
    Co-op - LAN         No
    Co-op - Online      No

Player counts:

    Online   2 players (1v1, exactly two per lobby)
    Local    2 players sharing one device (1v1)
