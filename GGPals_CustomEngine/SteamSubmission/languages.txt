ORDER N CHAOS - LANGUAGE SUPPORT

The game supports one language.

    LANGUAGE    INTERFACE (UI/HUD)    AUDIO (VOICE ACTING)    SUBTITLES
    English            Yes                    Yes                No

Notes:

    Interface   All menus, HUD, tutorial text, ability names, ability
                tooltips and the opening story sequence are in English.

    Audio       The game contains English announcer voice-over lines for
                deployment, planning, resolution, low health and victory.

    Subtitles   No. The announcer voice-over lines are not subtitled, so
                the game does not meet the requirement that EVERY spoken
                line has a subtitle. The opening story sequence is
                delivered as on-screen text and has no voice-over.
